Selected Projects

Research on Key Technologies for Vision-Guided Mobile Target Localization and Grasping in Water Bamboo Sorting Scenarios

Project Member, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications · 2025–2026

National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program

The team developed an automated water bamboo sorting system that combined vision-guided localization of moving conveyor-belt targets, quality classification, and robotic grasping. The project addressed challenges caused by irregular crop shapes, subtle appearance differences, target motion, and conveyor vibration.

The system used a Hikvision industrial camera and OpenCV for visual localization, with subpixel corner detection, PnP-based 3D pose estimation, Kalman-filter-based trajectory prediction, and vibration compensation. A lightweight ResNet18 model trained in PyTorch supported quality classification, while the integrated control pipeline coordinated visual recognition, localization, and grasping with an EFORT robotic arm.

My contributions included data organization, literature review, AI model experiments, technical documentation, and project presentations.
